Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

ShelfSpace is a personal reading-tracker application developed as an academic mini-project. This page explains what information the application collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, ShelfSpace receives your name, email address, and profile picture from Google, in order to identify your account and personalize your experience. We do not receive or request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, or any other Google service.

ShelfSpace also stores the information you choose to enter into the app: book and fanfiction entries, genres, tags, shelves, ratings, and notes you create.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and identify your personal account
  • To store and display your reading entries, shelves, and preferences, private to your account
  • To keep you signed in between visits

Data Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. Data is stored in a private database associated only with your account and is not visible to other users.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting the developer at the email below.

Contact

This is a student academic project. For any questions, please contact the developer via the project's GitHub repository.
